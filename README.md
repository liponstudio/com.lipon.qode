## ✅ Google Play Console — Trader status & Terms of Service

To clear the **"App-level trader status and Terms of Service"** task in Play Console:

1. **Trader status:** select **"Yes, my trader status applies to this app."**
   Qode is published for trade/business purposes (it serves ads and sells a one-time "remove ads" purchase), so the developer-account trader status correctly applies to this app. This keeps EEA/UK consumer-protection information accurate.
2. **Terms of Service URL:** paste the hosted terms page:
   ```
   https://liponstudio.github.io/com.lipon.qode/terms.html
   ```
   It is a standard, publicly accessible, non-editable HTML page with valid SSL (GitHub Pages), no geofencing or login, and it applies specifically to Qode.
3. **Save**, then send the change for review from **Publishing overview**.

> The terms page includes a dedicated **Trader status & consumer rights** section reflecting this declaration. If you ever stop monetising the app, revisit the trader declaration and section 3 of `terms.html`.
